<p>O capítulo apresenta o <em>plural</em> do acusativo, completando o
paradigma que começou em V:</p>
<table class="paradigm">
  <tr><th></th><th>nōm. sg.</th><th>acus. sg.</th><th>nōm. pl.</th><th>acus. pl.</th></tr>
  <tr><td class="label">m. (-us)</td><td>servus</td><td>servum</td><td>servī</td><td>serv<strong>ōs</strong></td></tr>
  <tr><td class="label">f. (-a)</td><td>via</td><td>viam</td><td>viae</td><td>vi<strong>ās</strong></td></tr>
  <tr><td class="label">n. (-um)</td><td>oppidum</td><td>oppidum</td><td>oppida</td><td>oppida</td></tr>
</table>
<p>O neutro, lembre-se, tem sempre <em>nōm. = acus.</em>: pl. <em>-a</em>.</p>
<div class="ex-block">
  Iūlius mult<strong>ōs</strong> serv<strong>ōs</strong> habet, et Aemilia mult<strong>ās</strong>
  ancill<strong>ās</strong>. — Inter Rōm<strong>am</strong> et Brundisi<strong>um</strong> est via Appia.
</div>
