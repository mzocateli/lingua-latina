<p>Cap. V completa o paradigma da 1ª/2ª declinação no plural:</p>
<table class="paradigm">
  <tr><th></th><th>nōm. sg.</th><th>acus. sg.</th><th>nōm. pl.</th><th>acus. pl.</th></tr>
  <tr><td class="label">m. (-us)</td><td>servus</td><td>servum</td><td>serv<strong>ī</strong></td><td>serv<strong>ōs</strong></td></tr>
  <tr><td class="label">f. (-a)</td><td>ancilla</td><td>ancillam</td><td>ancill<strong>ae</strong></td><td>ancill<strong>ās</strong></td></tr>
  <tr><td class="label">n. (-um)</td><td>cubiculum</td><td>cubiculum</td><td>cubicul<strong>a</strong></td><td>cubicul<strong>a</strong></td></tr>
</table>
<p>Note duas peculiaridades:</p>
<ul>
  <li>O <strong>neutro</strong> tem sempre <em>nōm. = acus.</em>: sg.
  <em>-um</em>, pl. <em>-a</em>.</li>
  <li>O <strong>feminino</strong> tem ambiguidade entre <em>ancilla</em>
  (nōm. sg.) e <em>ancilla</em> (nōm./acus. pl. neutro) — distingue-se
  pelo contexto e pelo macron de <em>ancillā</em> (abl. sg.).</li>
</ul>
<div class="ex-block">
  Iūlius mult<strong>ōs</strong> serv<strong>ōs</strong> habet; Aemilia
  mult<strong>ās</strong> ancill<strong>ās</strong>.<br>
  In hortō sunt mult<strong>ae</strong> ros<strong>ae</strong> et
  mult<strong>a</strong> līli<strong>a</strong>.
</div>
